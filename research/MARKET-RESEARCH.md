and measure; independent recruiters need a place to submit and get paid without chasing status by text. That is the gap CDL System occupies.

## 2. Competitive landscape
Three distinct models. CDL System does not fit cleanly into any one, which is both the positioning opportunity and a messaging risk.

### A. Agencies that sell recruiting to carriers (carrier is the buyer)
| Player | What the sources say | Pricing signal |
|---|---|---|
| **TransForce** | "Largest" CDL staffing/recruiting; claims 2M drivers in network, 3.5K dispatched daily, 30+ yrs — https://www.transforce.com/digital-recruiting (self-reported) | n/a |
| **Class A Recruiting** (Centerton, AR; classarecruitinginc.com) | Third-party listings: founded 2006, 21–150+ recruiters (listings disagree), "premier" CDL recruiting for drivers & carriers; revenue figures on aggregators are estimates and inconsistent — treat as unknown | unknown |
| **CDL Hunter** (Bentonville, AR) | Third-party recruiter; part of a network of 80–120 independent contract workers; ~100–120 drivers/month in Arkansas (2021 article); recruits independent recruiters (per-hire fee) — https://talkbusiness.net/2021/02/bentonville-truck-driver-recruiting-firm-releases-mobile-optimized-website/ | per-hire |
| **O Trucking** | Pay-per-placement: **$500/driver ($750/team)**, free replacement guarantee, 2–3 business-day shortlist — https://otrucking.com/careers/hire/ | $500 flat |
| **STS Recruiting** | Standard placement fee **$1,500** per company driver/owner-operator — https://form.jotform.com/262625356160051 | $1,500 |
| **Last Mile DR** | **$1,200** per placement paid in three milestones — https://lastmiledr.app/carriers-driver-recruiting | $1,200 milestone-billed |
| **Lanefinder** | "No-risk" pay-per-hire model — https://www.freightwaves.com/news/lanefinder-makes-no-risk-recruiting-a-reality-for-trucking-industry | pay-per-hire |
| Trucker Hunters, Leadgamp, Truck Driver Staffing (416 listed on noon.ai) | long tail of agencies | — |

Pricing benchmarks from aggregator guides (vendor content, treat as ranges): agencies 15–25% of first-year salary (~$9k–16k), flat fees $2,000–5,000, RPO $3,000–8,000/mo, job boards ≈ $2,000–5,000 effective per hire; replacement guarantees typically 60–90 days (O Trucking, Cargo Credible).
**Observation:** the small/mid agency tier is converging on **$500–$1,500 flat, pay-on-start, with a replacement guarantee** — that is the price anchor a carrier will compare CDL System's fee to.

### B. Recruiter networks that recruit recruiters (the recruiter is the customer)
Outlaw Trucking Group, Class A Jobs 411 / MMB Transportation (recruiters get **80% of placement commission**, "$1,000–$3,500+ per hire"), DriveLine Recruit, J Rose Logistics ("$500+ per placement"), Integrated Trucking Solution, Class A Solutions, M2Z Preferred Recruiters Network (return-to-duty drivers), CDL Hunter.
Pattern: 1099, commission-only, remote, "unlimited earning". Their pitch to recruiters is *income*; they say little about **status visibility, documents or follow-up ownership** — an angle CDL System can own.

### Where CDL System can differentiate (hypotheses to test)
1. **Both-sided pipeline with shared status** — recruiter and carrier see the same record. Agencies and ATSs each serve one side.
2. **"Not a job board; carriers decide"** — directly answers the carrier fear of lead-reselling (Avatar's criticism of Tenstreet) and the compliance fear of outsourcing the decision.
3. **Ownership of follow-up** — every step has an owner/date. Recruiter-network job ads don't promise it.
4. **Class A only, U.S. only** — narrow focus is credible next to TransForce-scale generalists.
5. **Pricing stays private (owner decision, D10)** — the $500–$1,500 anchors are context for the owner's own negotiations; CDL System publishes no fees and answers "agreed per partner".
Risks: a two-sided network has a cold-start problem (carriers need recruiters; recruiters need open offers); no public proof yet; "network" vs. "software" vs. "agency" can confuse — the hero copy resolves it as *network*.

## 3. Audience notes
- **Carrier recruiting manager / owner of a 10–100 truck fleet**: wants qualified Class A candidates without a monthly retainer; fears compliance exposure, no-shows, and lead resale. Searches: "driver recruiting services", "truck driver recruiting agency", "how to hire CDL drivers".
- **Independent recruiter (1099)**: commission-only; wants open offers, quick payouts, fewer status calls. Searches: "CDL recruiter jobs", "independent truck driver recruiter", "become a CDL recruiter".
- **Agency owner**: wants team visibility and a multi-carrier roster.
(Our existing recruiter structure from the owner's own business — recruiters/owners and 50-50 split — informs *payout design*, not public copy.)
