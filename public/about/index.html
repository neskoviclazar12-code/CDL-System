About CDL System

We run a CDL recruiting agency. This is what we built.

CDL System is built to connect carriers, independent recruiters, and agencies in one network.

Why we built it

Our agency ran drivers, carriers and follow-ups across spreadsheets, e-mails and phones. Good leads got lost because someone forgot to call. Two recruiters worked the same driver without knowing it. We stopped patching around it and built what we needed.

What it is today

A private network for three kinds of partners: carriers that hire CDL Class A drivers, independent recruiters who source them, and agencies that want more carriers to place with. Carriers make every hiring decision. CDL System is not a job board and does not employ drivers.
