How it works

Connect. Collaborate. Place.

CDL System is a network, not a job board. Carriers bring open positions, recruiters and agencies bring drivers, and every submission has a status and an owner.

The three stages

Connect, collaborate, place.

What each partner does at every stage, and what the platform keeps track of.

Carriers
Stages 1 and 3
Recruiters
Stages 1 and 2
Platform
All three

Carriers always make the hiring decision. CDL System does not employ drivers.

  1. Connect

    Carriers · Recruiting professionals

    Carriers share their open positions and what a qualified driver looks like for their operation: lanes, hiring states, experience and endorsements.

    Recruiting professionals apply to join the network. Accepted partners are onboarded and see the participating carrier opportunities available to them.

    In place
    Hiring needs, and the partners who can fill them

    For carriers

  2. Collaborate

    Recruiting professionals · Carriers

    Recruiting professionals source interested CDL Class A drivers, submit them to the carrier opportunity that fits, and keep each application's progress updated in one place.

    Carriers see each submission as it arrives, ask for what they need, and keep every update attached to the candidate instead of spread across inboxes.

    In place
    Every submission, with its status and updates

    For recruiters

  3. Place

    Carriers decide

    Carriers review candidates, run their own screening and safety approval, and make the hiring decision. The decision is always theirs.

    Recruiting professionals follow each placement through to the start, with compensation based on the partner terms agreed at onboarding.

    In place
    Hiring decisions and the placements that follow

    For agencies

Behind the network

Built for our own work first.

The platform came first, built for our own agency. It is where every submission lives, so everyone involved can tell where a driver stands. The roles stay simple: carriers decide, recruiting partners source and submit. How it works inside, we show partners on a call.
