Privacy policy

This policy describes what this website collects from visitors, why, who receives it, and how to see, correct or delete it. It covers the website and its forms. Participation in the CDL System network is governed by the partner agreement, not by this page.

Effective date: November 7, 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by CDL System LLC, reachable at info@cdlsystem.com and +1 224 765 8049.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the forms to add you to a marketing list. This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies. The only cookies, if any, are strictly necessary ones set by the web host to serve the site.

Who receives it

  • Our web host, which stores the website and its server logs.
  • Form submissions go to our e-mail inbox and are handled by the CDL System team responsible for that kind of request.

Phone calls, e-mails and text messages

We use the phone number and e-mail you give on a form to respond to that request. We do not send marketing text messages because you completed an inquiry form. Text messages are sent only if you ticked the separate SMS consent box, and only about your request; the SMS terms describe that program and how to stop it.

How long we keep it

We keep a form submission only as long as needed to respond to it and, where a partnership follows, for the duration of that relationship and the period required by law afterwards. Server logs are kept by the web host for its standard period.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, e-mail info@cdlsystem.com. We respond within 30 days. California residents have the rights described in the California Consumer Privacy Act; the request route is the same.

Do Not Track

This site does not change its behavior in response to "Do Not Track" browser signals.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

When this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and material changes are announced on this page.
