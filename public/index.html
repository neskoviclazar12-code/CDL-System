The platform

Built by recruiters, for recruiting.

CDL System runs on a platform we built for our own agency first. It is the reason the network works the way it does. We would rather show it to you than describe it.

Industry focus

U.S. trucking. CDL Class A. Nothing else.

CDL System works in one industry and speaks its language: carriers, endorsements, home time, orientation, first load. The network is built for trucking companies that hire CDL Class A drivers and for the recruiting professionals who know how to find them, so every conversation starts from the same understanding of the job.

Carriers keep responsibility for reviewing applicants, verifying qualifications, running safety and compliance checks, and making the final hiring decision. CDL System is not a job board and does not employ drivers.

About CDL System

Built from real recruiting work, not a whiteboard.

CDL System grew out of the day-to-day work of a CDL recruiting agency: the calls that were missed, the drivers two recruiters worked without knowing it, the applications nobody could find the status of. Each of those problems shaped how the network and its platform work today.
