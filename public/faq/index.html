FAQ

Questions, answered.

Short answers to the questions we hear most. For anything else, ask us.

What is CDL System?

CDL System is built to connect carriers, independent recruiters, and agencies in one network. It was built by people who run a CDL recruiting agency, and it is a private network for CDL Class A hiring in the U.S.

Is CDL System a job board?

No. We do not post jobs for drivers to browse, and we do not employ drivers. Recruiters and agencies submit drivers to participating carriers, and the carriers decide.

Who makes the hiring decision?

The carrier, always. Screening, safety checks, compliance checks and the final hiring decision stay with the carrier.

Who can join the network?

Trucking carriers that hire CDL Class A drivers, independent recruiters who source them, and recruiting agencies. Every application is reviewed individually, so not everyone who applies is accepted.

Which drivers do you work with?

CDL Class A drivers, for U.S. trucking. That is the only kind of hiring we do.

What does it cost?

Terms are agreed with each partner. Tell us what you need and we will reply with details.

How do I get started?

Click Partner with us, tell us whether you are a carrier, a recruiter or an agency, and send a short inquiry. We reply within one business day to set up a call.

Do you text drivers?

Only with recorded consent. See our SMS terms.
